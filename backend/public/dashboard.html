Cards for Routing

When you pay with your SmartCard, GeoRewards automatically routes the charge to whichever card earns you the most rewards — if the reward beats our routing cost. The routing fee comes out of your rewards balance, so you always come out ahead.

How it works
1. You pay with SmartCard at Domino's (MCC 5814 — Fast Food)
2. We check your routing cards — your Chase earns 3% on dining
3. 3% − 1.5% SmartCard base = 1.5% extra. On $20 that's $0.30 extra
4. Routing fee (2.9% + $0.30 = $0.88) vs. benefit ($0.30) → no route
5. On larger amounts, routing wins — e.g. $200 pizza: $2.22 extra vs $6.08 fee → still no
6. Your card earns 5% on travel: $200 flight → $7 extra vs $6.08 fee → routed ✓
